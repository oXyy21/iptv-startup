# Top 10 4K Movies to Stream This Weekend

So, you just upgraded your subscription and you've got a brand new 4K OLED TV. What should you watch to truly test out the visual fidelity of your setup?

Here is our curated list of visually breathtaking movies available right now in our VOD library.

### 1. Dune: Part Two
The sweeping dunes of Arrakis look absolutely incredible in uncompressed 4K. The contrast between the deep spice sands and the harsh sun is a perfect test for HDR.

### 2. Blade Runner 2049
A modern masterpiece of cinematography. The neon-drenched streets of Los Angeles will push your TV's color accuracy and deep blacks to their limits.

### 3. Mad Max: Fury Road
Explosions, chrome, and chaos. The fast-paced action and vibrant orange-and-teal color grading make this a must-watch in 4K.

### 4. Interstellar
Experience the vastness of space. The IMAX scenes, particularly the water planet and the black hole, are jaw-dropping in high definition.

### 5. Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse
The unique, comic-book art style pops off the screen in 4K. Every frame is a painting, and the high bitrate of our VOD servers ensures no compression artifacts during fast motion.
