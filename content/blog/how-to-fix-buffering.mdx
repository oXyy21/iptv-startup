# How to Fix IPTV Buffering

There is nothing more frustrating than your stream freezing right as your favorite team is about to score. While our service features powerful Anti-Freeze technology, local network issues can still cause buffering. 

Follow this guide to ensure a flawless streaming experience.

## Step 1: Check Your Internet Speed
IPTV, especially 4K streams, requires a stable connection. You need a minimum of **25 Mbps** for standard viewing, but **50 Mbps+** is recommended for uninterrupted 4K streaming. Run a speed test to verify your bandwidth.

## Step 2: Use an Ethernet Cable
Wi-Fi is convenient, but it is highly susceptible to interference from walls, microwaves, and other devices. **Hardwire your device** (Smart TV, Android Box, PC) directly to your router using a Cat6 Ethernet cable. This single step solves 90% of buffering issues.

## Step 3: Use a Premium VPN
Did you know that many Internet Service Providers (ISPs) actively throttle IPTV traffic? If your speed test is fast but your stream is buffering, your ISP might be to blame. Use a trusted VPN (like ExpressVPN or NordVPN) to encrypt your traffic and bypass ISP throttling.

## Step 4: Clear Device Cache
If you're using an Android Box or a Firestick, your device's memory might be full. Go to your app settings and clear the cache of your IPTV player app. Restart the device.

By following these steps, you'll ensure that you get the absolute most out of our premium server network. Happy streaming!
